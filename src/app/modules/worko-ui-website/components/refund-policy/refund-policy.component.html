Refund Policy

At Worko.ai, we are committed to providing a seamless and satisfactory experience for our users. Our refund policy is designed to address specific scenarios related to the purchase of our premium packages.

1. Mistaken Purchase

  • If a user has mistakenly purchased a premium package and wishes to request a refund, they must contact us at Refund@worko.ai within 6 hours of the purchase. To be eligible for a refund, the user must not have initiated any referral requests during this period. Once the request is received within the stipulated time frame, we will process the refund promptly.

2. Post 6 Hours or Referral Request Initiation

  • Refund requests made after the initial 6 hours of the premium package purchase or if the user has already requested a referral using the premium subscription will not be processed. Once a referral request has been initiated, the premium subscription is considered utilized, and the refund option is no longer applicable.

How to Request a Refund

  • To request a refund, please contact our support team at Refund@worko.ai. Include the relevant details of your purchase, the reason for the refund, and any supporting information. Our team will review the request and respond within a reasonable time frame.

Contact Information

  • For any queries or concerns related to refunds, please reach out to us at:
    Email: Refund@worko.ai
  • Note: Worko.ai reserves the right to modify or update the refund policy at any time without prior notice. Users are encouraged to review the policy periodically to stay informed about any changes.
